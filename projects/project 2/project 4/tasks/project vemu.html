About VEMU Institute of Technology

VEMU Institute of Technology, established in 2001, is one of the leading engineering colleges in Andhra Pradesh. The institute emphasizes academic excellence, research, innovation, and holistic student development. The campus features modern classrooms, advanced labs, sports facilities, and a library with thousands of reference books. Our mission is to produce skilled engineers who contribute positively to society.
