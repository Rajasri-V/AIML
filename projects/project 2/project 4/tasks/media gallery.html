About Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli is a famous Indian cricketer and one of the greatest batsmen in the world. He was born on 5 November 1988 in Delhi, India. He plays for the Indian national cricket team and is known for his aggressive batting style and strong leadership qualities.

Virat Kohli has served as the captain of India in all formats of cricket and led the team to many historic victories. He has scored numerous centuries in Test, ODI, and T20 formats. He is also the former captain of Royal Challengers Bangalore (RCB) in the Indian Premier League (IPL).

Apart from cricket, Virat Kohli is a fitness icon and youth role model. He promotes healthy living and is involved in various social and charitable activities.
